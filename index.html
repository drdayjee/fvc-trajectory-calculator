This tool is an early FVC trajectory probability calculator based on a multinomial logistic model that uses baseline FVC % predicted, change in FVC % predicted, and a 6-month indicator. Probabilities are computed with a numerically stable softmax against a reference category.

For informational and research use only. This calculator does not provide a clinical diagnosis and does not replace clinical judgment, multidisciplinary discussion, or formal evaluation by a qualified clinician.
