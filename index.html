Skip to calculator Early FVC Trajectory Probability Calculator Estimates the probability of belonging to one of four early FVC decline trajectory groups in interstitial lung disease, based on baseline FVC % predicted and a follow-up measurement. Patient inputs Baseline FVC (% predicted) Initial measurement. Decimals allowed. Follow-up FVC (% predicted) Repeat measurement at 3 or 6 months. Interval (months) — Select — 3 months 6 months Time between the two measurements. Calculate Reset Results Enter values and select Calculate to view trajectory probabilities. The predicted group corresponds to the trajectory with the highest posterior probability. Confidence is labelled High-confidence when the top probability is ≥ 0.70, Probable when 0.50–0.69, and Indeterminate below 0.50. For research and educational use. This tool supports interpretation of early FVC trajectory and is not a substitute for clinical judgment, multidisciplinary review, or evaluation by a qualified clinician.
